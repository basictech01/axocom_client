# **DEV BHOOMI AI SUMMIT 2026**

## **Overview**

The **DevBhoomi AI Summit 2026** is envisioned as a high-level platform bringing together government leadership, policymakers, technology experts, AI practitioners, industry leaders, innovators, startups, academia and other stakeholders to explore the transformative potential of Artificial Intelligence in Uttarakhand.

The Summit focuses on the practical and responsible application of AI across governance, public administration, disaster management, environment and forests, smart cities, public safety, citizen services, data governance and administrative efficiency.

The Summit seeks to create a meaningful dialogue between government and the technology ecosystem, with a particular emphasis on how emerging technologies can be translated into practical solutions for real-world challenges and contribute to a more efficient, responsive and technology-enabled Uttarakhand.

Rather than limiting the conversation around Artificial Intelligence to technological advancement alone, the Summit is positioned around the broader question of how AI can improve governance, public service delivery, institutional efficiency, citizen experience and sustainable development.

The event will provide a common platform for government representatives, technology companies, AI experts, startups, innovators, researchers, policymakers and industry stakeholders to exchange knowledge, showcase solutions, discuss emerging opportunities and explore potential collaborations.

## **Vision and Objectives**

The DevBhoomi AI Summit 2026 aims to contribute to the development of an AI-enabled ecosystem in Uttarakhand by bringing together the public sector, private sector, technology ecosystem, academia and innovation community on a common platform.

The Summit seeks to encourage conversations that move beyond theoretical discussions of AI and focus on its practical relevance to governance, public administration, citizen services, infrastructure, environment, public safety and other areas of importance to the State.

It aims to create an environment where government challenges can be discussed alongside technological capabilities, enabling stakeholders to identify opportunities for collaboration, innovation and implementation.

The Summit's principal objectives are to:

* Bring government and technology stakeholders together around the opportunities presented by Artificial Intelligence.  
* Explore practical applications of AI in governance and public administration.  
* Discuss how AI can improve administrative efficiency and citizen services.  
* Examine the role of AI in disaster management and public safety.  
* Explore applications of AI for environment and forest management.  
* Discuss AI-enabled approaches to smart cities and urban development.  
* Promote conversations around responsible and effective data governance.  
* Provide visibility to AI companies, technology organisations, startups and innovators.  
* Facilitate interaction between policymakers, government officials, industry leaders, technology experts and innovators.  
* Encourage technology-led solutions to real-world challenges.  
* Create opportunities for partnerships, knowledge exchange, technology showcases and stakeholder networking.

## **Panel Discussion Agendas**

The Summit will feature panel discussions on the application of Artificial Intelligence to areas with direct relevance to governance and public life.

The panel discussion agendas are:

### **AI for Governance**

Exploring how Artificial Intelligence can support government decision-making, policy implementation, institutional processes and governance systems.

### **AI for Public Administration**

Examining how AI can help public institutions improve administrative processes, efficiency, resource utilisation, monitoring and decision-support mechanisms.

### **AI for Disaster Management**

Exploring the potential of AI, data analytics and emerging technologies in disaster preparedness, prediction, response, risk assessment and management.

This is particularly relevant to Uttarakhand given the State's geographical and environmental characteristics and the need for effective technology-enabled disaster preparedness and response.

### **AI for Environment & Forests**

Examining how AI and data-driven technologies can support environmental monitoring, forest management, conservation, ecological assessment and sustainable development.

### **AI for Smart Cities**

Exploring AI-enabled approaches to urban management, infrastructure, mobility, public utilities, planning and service delivery.

### **AI for Public Safety**

Discussing applications of Artificial Intelligence and emerging technologies for improving public safety, monitoring, emergency response and institutional coordination.

### **AI for Citizen Services**

Exploring how AI can improve the accessibility, speed, efficiency and responsiveness of public services and enhance citizen interaction with government systems.

### **AI for Data Governance**

Discussing the importance of effective data management, governance, security, accessibility and responsible use of data as AI adoption increases across public institutions.

### **AI for Administrative Efficiency**

Exploring how AI can assist institutions in automating repetitive processes, improving workflows, supporting decision-making and enabling more efficient administration.

## **Speaking and Panel Participation Opportunities**

The DevBhoomi AI Summit 2026 welcomes expressions of interest from experienced professionals, practitioners and subject-matter experts who would like to contribute to the Summit as a **speaker, panellist, moderator or session contributor**.

Individuals with relevant expertise in any of the panel discussion agendas outlined above may apply for an opportunity to speak or participate as a panellist in the corresponding discussion. Speaking opportunities are intended for individuals who can add practical knowledge, informed perspectives, relevant case studies or implementation experience. Suitable applicants may include:

* Government and public-sector leaders
* AI and emerging-technology experts
* Founders, senior industry leaders and technology executives
* Researchers, academicians and public-policy specialists
* Startup and innovation ecosystem leaders
* Professionals with relevant implementation experience
* Representatives of organisations with credible AI solutions or use cases

Individuals interested in participating should submit an **expression of interest to the Summit organising team** containing:

* A brief professional profile and current designation
* The proposed topic and preferred panel agenda
* A short summary of the perspective, case study or expertise they would contribute
* Links to relevant work, publications, projects or previous speaking engagements, where applicable
* Any preferred participation format, such as keynote, presentation, panel discussion, fireside chat or moderation

The organising team will review submissions according to their relevance to the Summit themes, depth of expertise, practical value for delegates, diversity of perspective and fit within the developing programme. Shortlisted applicants may be contacted for further discussion before a speaking or panel role is confirmed.

Organisations participating through applicable partnership categories may also receive a keynote, panel, demonstration or knowledge-session opportunity, subject to the deliverables of the selected category and final programme approval.

As session capacity is limited, submitting an expression of interest or becoming a delegate does not automatically guarantee a speaking position. All speaker roles, panel compositions, topics and formats remain subject to curation, availability and confirmation by the organising team.
